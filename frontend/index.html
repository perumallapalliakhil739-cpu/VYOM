Remote Sensing Radiative Physics & Atmospheric Penetration

How spaceborne sensors detect active thermal hotspots through dense smoke and mitigate cloud cover

NASA FIRMS VIIRS & MODIS Science Protocols
💨

Smoke Penetration & Mie Scattering Physics

Wildfire smoke is composed of microscopic aerosol droplets (0.1–0.8 µm) that severely scatter human visible light (0.4–0.7 µm). However, NASA's thermal sensors observe in the Mid-Wave Infrared (MWIR) at 3.7–4.0 µm (VIIRS Band I4 & MODIS Band 21/22).

Visible Light (0.5 µm): Blocked (~15% transmission)
Mid-Wave Infrared (3.9 µm): Transparent (~98.5% transmission)
Wien's Displacement Law: Wildfires burn at 800–1200 K, placing their peak electromagnetic energy emission directly at 3.9 µm. Even small flames occupying just 0.01% of a 375m pixel elevate the MWIR temperature by tens of Kelvins above ambient ground.
📐

Dual-Band Contextual Algorithm (ΔT)

To prevent false positives from sun-warmed rocks or solar glint, satellites compute the contextual brightness temperature difference between the flame-sensitive 4 µm channel and the ambient 11 µm channel:

ΔT = T4µm - T11µm
  • Normal Terrain / Smoke: T4µm ≈ T11µm (ΔT is small, < 5–8 K).
  • Active Wildfire: T4µm surges drastically while T11µm only rises slightly (ΔT spikes > 15–30 K).
  • Adaptive Background Test: The candidate pixel is statistically tested against surrounding valid pixels to eliminate local temperature anomalies.
☁️

Cloud Obscuration Protocol & Masking

Thick water/ice clouds contain droplets measuring 10–50 µm that physically absorb and scatter infrared radiation. Optical and IR satellites cannot see through dense stratus or storm clouds:

  • 1.38 µm Cirrus Band: Strong water vapor absorption flags thin, high-altitude cirrus clouds that might distort thermal readings.
  • Cloud-Mask Flagging: Pixels under dense cloud decks are marked as Cloud-Obscured in FIRMS rather than "No Fire", ensuring forecasting models do not misinterpret cloudy days as extinguished fires.
  • Sub-Pixel Burn Scars: Post-fire thermal radiance and surface reflectance changes are verified as soon as cloud fronts pass.
🛰️
